## Consent checkpoint

Before a session, explain purpose, duration, recording choice, voluntary participation, data retention, and the instruction not to expose confidential note content. Record only:

```text
Participant ID:
Segment:
Platform/device band:
Consent to notes: yes/no
Consent to recording: yes/no
Retention/deletion date:
```

## Discovery prompts

1. Show how you last captured something without an obvious destination.
2. Show how you found information you vaguely remembered.
3. Describe the last conflict, lost edit, or restore attempt.
4. Explain what “local,” “private,” “backup,” and “sync” mean to you.
5. Show which note-tool capabilities or plugins you consider indispensable.
6. What evidence would you require before downloading and using a local model?
7. What would AI have to show before you approved changes to several notes?

Avoid asking whether a participant “likes” a proposed feature when behavior can be observed.
